Tax policy notice

Insert-only policy: Under GST compliance, tax rates tied to existing batches and past invoices cannot be altered or deleted.

When a government GST council updates the slab rate for a commodity, a new HSN entry is registered in the database. New stock deliveries are recorded under the new rate, while historical invoices preserve the original rate at the time of transaction.
